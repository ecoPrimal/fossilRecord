## 🎯 **Key Concepts**

### Modern Capability-Based Architecture

Find services by **what they do**, not where they are:

```rust
// Capability-based discovery
let discovery = CapabilityAwareDiscovery::initialize(&config).await?;
let api_services = discovery.find_service("api").await?;
```

### Self-Knowledge Pattern

Primals discover themselves - no configuration needed:
- ✅ Ports assigned by OS (`bind("0.0.0.0:0")`)
- ✅ Services discovered at runtime
- ✅ Zero hardcoding

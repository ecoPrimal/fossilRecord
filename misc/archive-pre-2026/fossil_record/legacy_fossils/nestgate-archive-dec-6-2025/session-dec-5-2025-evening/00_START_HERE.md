## 💡 KEY INSIGHT

**Modern Capability-Based Architecture** means:
- Services find each other by **capability**, not hardcoded address
- Primals **discover themselves** before discovering peers
- **Zero hardcoded** ports, hosts, or endpoints
- **Runtime discovery** for everything

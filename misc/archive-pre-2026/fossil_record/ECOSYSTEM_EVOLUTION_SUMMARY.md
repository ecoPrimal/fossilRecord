## 🧬 **Biological Accuracy**

This evolution aligns with **real ecological science**:

### **Symbiotic Relationships**
- **Mutualistic**: Both parties benefit (Squirrel ↔ Songbird coordination)
- **Commensal**: One benefits, other neutral (ToadStool → NestGate compute/storage)
- **Facilitative**: One helps the other thrive (BearDog → ecosystem security)

### **Niche Specialization**
- Each primal occupies distinct ecological niches
- Prevents direct competition while enabling complementary capabilities
- Mirrors how different species occupy different trophic levels

### **Succession Patterns**
- **Pioneer species**: Basic primals establish initial environment
- **Climax community**: Full ecosystem with all specialized primals
- **Disturbance recovery**: Automatic recolonization after failures

## 🎓 **LESSONS LEARNED**

### **1. Verify Claims with Measurements**
- Don't trust documentation without verification
- Run actual tools (tarpaulin, clippy, etc.)
- Measure regularly, not just once

### **2. Distinguish Aspirational from Actual**
- Specs can be goals, not current state
- Mark clearly: "Target" vs "Current"
- Don't promote goals to achievements

### **3. Regular Ecosystem Audits**
- Re-verify all primals quarterly
- Catch documentation drift early
- Maintain accurate cross-primal view

### **4. Honest Communication**
- Better to have realistic timelines than miss them
- False confidence leads to planning failures
- Stakeholders appreciate honesty
